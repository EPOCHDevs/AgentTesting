# Audit scope

Treat the selected vendor's data and declared basis as authoritative. Vendors
own their data cleaning, standardization and corrections.

Audit our source selection, bindings, units, transformations, calculations and
delivered results against those inputs. Check genuine missing observations and
platform failures. Do not second-guess vendor values through external filings,
invented accounting reconciliations or inferred quarter/YTD conversions. A
difference from another source is not itself an Epoch failure.

Preserve original attempt artifacts. Record a revised interpretation as a new
review; do not overwrite evidence or describe a reassessment as a fresh run.
