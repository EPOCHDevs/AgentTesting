# Industrial resilience

**Role:** An industrial equities analyst preparing a long-term watchlist discussion.
Their team wants to understand business resilience before spending time on a
full investment case.

**Opening question**

> I'm comparing Fastenal and MSC Industrial for a long-term watchlist. Which business looks better able to withstand a slowdown in customer orders, and what makes you think so?

**What matters to this person:** Evidence from the businesses, the comparability
of their operations, and where an apparent advantage could weaken.
