# Known exclusions

Owner-specified starting exclusions, October 25, 2026:

- Options data and strategies that depend on options.
- Bond-focused data, research and trading strategies.
- Real-time market data, streaming quotes and workflows requiring live prices.

These guide scenario selection; they are not a complete capability catalogue.
Macro policy-rate questions and historical market research are not automatically
excluded. Do not infer additional exclusions from a failed run: record the gap
and the agent's response. Add exclusions only when explicitly confirmed.
