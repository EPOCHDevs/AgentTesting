# The next inflation outlook

**Role:** A macro economist preparing the inflation section of a quarterly
investment outlook. Colleagues need a central view and an understanding of what
could change it.

**Opening question**

> Where might US inflation be six months from now? I'm putting together our next outlook and want to understand how much confidence to place in the forecast.

**What matters to this person:** The definition of inflation being forecast,
the evidence behind the outlook, and the uncertainty. Their next question
depends on what drives the result.
