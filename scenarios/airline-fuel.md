# Fuel and airline performance

**Role:** An airline planning analyst preparing background for an operating-cost
discussion. Southwest is the public-company reference for the review.

**Opening question**

> I'm reviewing Southwest's exposure to fuel costs. Across past reporting periods, how much did changes in fuel prices affect operating performance?

**What matters to this person:** Distinguishing fuel's contribution from other
changes in costs and revenue, and understanding how closely the price evidence
matches what Southwest actually paid.
