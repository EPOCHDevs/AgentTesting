# Research situations

Use the [workflow coverage programme](coverage/README.md) to choose release
prerequisites and deeper cases. It maps current capabilities, meaningful workflow
variants, edge cases and review evidence. The six briefs below are starting
conversations; they are not the full platform acceptance checklist.

Start with a person's work, not a list of Epoch features. These are fictional
composite roles, not accounts of real clients or claims about market conditions.

| Brief | Role | Research need |
|---|---|---|
| [Industrial resilience](industrial-resilience.md) | Equity analyst | Compare how two businesses might withstand weaker orders |
| [A simpler equity ride](equity-trend.md) | Systematic researcher | Investigate a simple trend strategy after costs |
| [The next inflation outlook](inflation-outlook.md) | Macro economist | Develop a forecast and understand its uncertainty |
| [Euro revenue exposure](euro-revenue.md) | Corporate treasury analyst | Reassess the relationship between exchange rates and interest rates |
| [Fuel and airline performance](airline-fuel.md) | Airline planning analyst | Understand a material operating-cost exposure |
| [Diversifying technology exposure](equity-diversification.md) | Family office analyst | Assess a proposed geographic diversification |

Give the acting researcher one brief. Send its opening question to Epoch, then
read the actual answer before deciding what to ask next. The brief explains
motivation; it is not extra text to paste into every request. There is no scripted
sequence or quota of turns. Answer clarifications from the persona's context.
Follow the evidence even when it contradicts the opening premise or produces no
clear conclusion. Stop when the researcher has what they need or further
conversation would add little.

Keep only the known exclusions in [EXCLUSIONS.md](EXCLUSIONS.md). Other missing
data or capabilities are findings. Preserve the original ask and record how
Epoch explains the gap, asks for a decision, or proposes an alternative. A
substitute must be explicit; it must not quietly become the original task.

A separate reviewer checks the evidence and whether the research need was met.
An honest limitation is useful evidence even when the need remains unmet; it
does not pass a required positive workflow. Select planned coverage from the
programme before a campaign, then record which capabilities and depth stages were
actually exercised afterward. Use roles, questions and markets to diversify
assignments; let follow-ups arise from the evidence and the person's decision.
