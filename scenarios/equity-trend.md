# A simpler equity ride

**Role:** A systematic researcher at a small asset manager, investigating a
simple alternative to holding a US equity ETF through every drawdown. The
mandate allows the ETF and cash, with no leverage or short selling.

**Opening question**

> Would a simple trend rule on a broad US equity ETF have made the ride easier over the past ten years, once trading costs are included?

**What matters to this person:** The trade-off between drawdowns, missed gains
and trading activity. They want a rule they can explain to colleagues, and would
be interested in whether a promising result survives a different market period.
