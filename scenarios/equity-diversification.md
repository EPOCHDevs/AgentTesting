# Diversifying technology exposure

**Role:** A family office analyst preparing an initial discussion about geographic
concentration. This is an initial comparison of listed equity exposures;
allocation sizing comes later.

**Opening question**

> Would adding a broad Japan equity fund meaningfully diversify a Nasdaq-100 allocation, or would they still suffer at the same times?

**What matters to this person:** How the exposures behave together during
difficult periods, what currency exposure changes, and whether a result depends
on a particular window. They need to understand the comparison before discussing
how much to allocate.
