# Euro revenue exposure

**Role:** A treasury analyst at a US manufacturer that receives part of its
revenue in euros and reports in dollars. They are reviewing the currency
assumptions used in quarterly budgets.

**Opening question**

> Is the US–eurozone interest-rate gap still a useful guide to EUR/USD, or has that relationship changed?

**What matters to this person:** Whether the relationship is stable enough to
inform a budget discussion, when it has broken down, and what the available
evidence can actually establish.
